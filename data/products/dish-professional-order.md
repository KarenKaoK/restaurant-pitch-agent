# DISH Professional Order

## Official facts

- **What it is:** Online ordering for takeaway and delivery.
- **Key features:**
  - Orders come in through the restaurant's own website or Google
  - Owner controls menu, prices, availability and promotions
  - Common online payment methods (e.g. PayPal)
  - Optional mobile card reader and receipt printer, covering the process from order to delivery
- **Problems it addresses:** Per-order commissions charged by delivery platforms, and handling transactions from order to delivery.
- **Pricing model:** Transparent fixed monthly price, no per-order commission.
- **Related:** DISH Premium bundles it with the website and Professional Reservation. DISH POS also offers an Order Aggregator (pulls in orders from platforms like Lieferando and Wolt) and Order2POS (click & collect webshop).

## Sales notes (project-authored, not from DISH)

- **Pain signals in reviews:**
  - "No online ordering, had to call for takeaway"
  - "Phone line busy when ordering takeaway"
  - "Only on delivery apps, prices higher than in the restaurant"
  - Guests asking for takeaway or delivery that isn't offered online
- **Good fit:** Pizzerias, Asian restaurants, burger places and other takeaway-heavy concepts.
- **Weak fit:** Fine dining or places that don't offer takeaway at all.
- **Possible objection:** "I'm already on a delivery platform." → Use own channel alongside platforms to keep orders commission-free.
