# DISH POS

## Official facts

- **What it is:** A cloud-based POS (point-of-sale) system for restaurants, cafés, bars and other catering businesses. Orders are managed centrally, bills can be split, and sales and stock are visible in real time.
- **Hardware:**
  - Stationary touch display
  - Optional handheld for taking orders at the table (readable in sunlight, suits terraces)
  - Printers, cash drawers and scanners controlled from any POS device
  - Mini server that keeps the system running offline and syncs to the cloud when back online
- **Key features:**
  - Works during internet outages
  - Easy touchscreen use, little training needed
  - Fast price changes and offers (e.g. daily menu)
  - Table planning and order history
  - Real-time sales and stock data
  - QR code ordering and self-checkout
  - DATEV export for accounting; records compliant with German rules (GoBD, TSE, DSFinV-K)
- **Problems it addresses:** Wrong bills, mixed-up orders, time lost on taking orders and handling bills.
- **Pricing (Germany, excl. VAT, as of 2026-11-24):** Hardware and installation from EUR 1,684 (minimal setup); POS licence EUR 69/month.
- **Add-ons:** QR Ordering (guests scan to order and pay), Bar Kitchen Manager, Order Aggregator (Lieferando, Wolt), Order2POS (click & collect). Integrates with DISH Pay.

## Sales notes (project-authored, not from DISH)

- **Pain signals in reviews:**
  - "Our order was wrong" / "forgot part of our order"
  - "Waited ages for the waiter to take our order"
  - "The bill was wrong" / "charged for things we didn't order"
  - "Couldn't split the bill"
  - Slow service on a busy terrace
- **Good fit:** Busy restaurants with table service and visible ordering or billing errors.
- **Weak fit:** Very small places where reviews show no ordering or billing issues.
- **Note:** "Slow service" alone is ambiguous: check whether reviews point to ordering (POS / handheld) or paying (Pay).
- **Possible objection:** "A new POS is expensive." → Monthly licence; fewer errors and faster table turns.
