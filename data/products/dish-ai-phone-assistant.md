# AI Phone Assistant

## Official facts

- **What it is:** A paid add-on for DISH Professional Reservation and DISH Premium customers. It answers phone calls and creates reservations in DISH Reservation. Provided together with the partner Assistant AI.
- **How it works:** When staff can't pick up (busy service or closed), calls are forwarded to a dedicated number; the assistant collects the booking details and enters the reservation in DISH.
- **Availability:** Pilot in Germany and Austria only.
- **Prerequisite:** An active DISH Professional Reservation or DISH Premium subscription.
- **Pricing (as listed on the DISH support site, 2026-11-24):** EUR 99 one-time activation, EUR 9.90/month, plus EUR 0.35 per accepted call. Cancellable monthly (two separate contracts).
- **Current limitations:**
  - Cannot handle bookings that need a credit card guarantee
  - Cannot accept bookings that would become a reservation request
  - Cannot change existing reservations
  - In these cases, the call is forwarded to the restaurant

## Sales notes (project-authored, not from DISH)

- **Pain signals in reviews:**
  - "Nobody ever answers the phone" (especially during service hours)
  - "Called during opening hours, no answer, gave up"
  - Many guests still prefer to call instead of booking online
- **How it differs from Professional Reservation alone:** Reservation moves bookings online; the phone assistant covers guests who still call. If reviews only mention "no online booking", recommend Reservation. If they mention unanswered calls, Reservation + AI Phone Assistant can be suggested.
- **Do not recommend on its own:** it requires Professional Reservation.
