# DISH Starter

## Official facts

- **What it is:** A website builder for restaurants.
- **Key features:**
  - Mobile-friendly restaurant website, built in minutes without technical skills
  - Digital menu with allergen labels; the menu can be edited at any time
  - Built-in reservation widget
  - Google Business Profile setup, so the restaurant shows up in Google Search and Maps
- **Problems it addresses:** Restaurants with no or weak online presence that guests struggle to find.
- **Pricing model:** One-time fixed price; no subscription and no commission.
- **Related:** DISH Premium bundles a professional website with Professional Reservation and Professional Order, plus Google profile and review management, automated SEO and AI translation of website text.

## Sales notes (project-authored, not from DISH)

- **Pain signals in reviews:**
  - "Couldn't find a menu online" / "no website"
  - "Opening hours on Google were wrong"
  - "Hard to find", "didn't know this place existed"
  - Menu or allergen info only available by asking staff
- **Good fit:** Small independent places with little online presence.
- **Weak fit:** Restaurants that already have a good website and accurate Google profile.
- **Possible objection:** "I don't have time to maintain a website." → Setup takes minutes and the menu can be edited from the dashboard.
