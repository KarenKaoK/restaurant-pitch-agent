# DISH Pay / DISH Pay Now

## Official facts

### DISH Pay

- **What it is:** An all-in-one cashless payment solution for hospitality. Integrates with DISH POS so ordering and payment work together.
- **Payment methods:** Contactless card or phone payments (card reader or tap-to-pay), QR code payments (self-service, takeaway), online payments through an ordering system.
- **Terminals:** Tap to Pay handheld (order taking and payment in one device), V400c Plus, S1F2 (mobile receipt printing, switches between Wi-Fi and SIM).
- **Features:** Tipping at the terminal, transparent fees, regular payouts, an app to monitor transactions.
- **Problems it addresses:** Reliance on cash, slow payment, unclear finances.
- **Pricing (Germany, excl. VAT, as of 2026-11-24):** Transaction fees from 1.89% (depends on card type). Terminals can be rented (e.g. V400c Plus EUR 12.90/month on a 60-month term) or bought (e.g. V400c Plus EUR 399).

### DISH Pay Now

- **What it is:** A small portable card reader with no monthly fee and no contract. Needs only Wi-Fi.
- **Features:** Major cards and mobile wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), tipping, digital receipts by QR code or email, online dashboard, payout usually within two business days.
- **Good for:** Businesses with seasonal or occasional needs that want no fixed costs.
- **Pricing (Germany, as of 2026-11-24):** Device EUR 39 one-time (recommended price), transaction fee from 1.29% (excl. VAT).

## Sales notes (project-authored, not from DISH)

- **Pain signals in reviews:**
  - "Cash only" / "no card payment"
  - "Card machine didn't work"
  - "Had to go to an ATM"
  - "Waited a long time for the bill" / "paying took forever"
- **Which one to suggest:**
  - Small, low-volume or seasonal place → DISH Pay Now (no monthly cost)
  - Restaurant with table service or using DISH POS → DISH Pay
- **Possible objection:** "Card fees are too high." → Transparent fees; guests who can pay by card spend and tip more easily (frame as an argument, not a DISH claim).
