# DISH Professional Reservation

## Official facts

- **What it is:** An online table reservation system for restaurants.
- **Key features:**
  - Guests book 24/7 through the restaurant's website or directly from Google
  - Widget that can be added to any website
  - Drag-and-drop table management, seating areas, live changes to reservations
  - Capacity control for peak times and events
  - Automatic confirmations, plus email and SMS reminders to reduce no-shows
- **Problems it addresses:** Time lost answering phone bookings, manual reservation handling, double bookings and no-shows.
- **Pricing model (Germany, excl. VAT, as of 2026-11-24):**
  - EUR 39/month (36-month contract), EUR 49/month (12 months), EUR 54/month (3 months)
  - One-time setup fee EUR 69
  - 0% commission per reservation
- **Add-ons:**
  - **SMS Notification Package:** up to three SMS per booking (confirmation, reminder, cancellation)
  - **No-Show Card Guarantee:** guests leave card details when booking; the restaurant can charge a preset fee for no-shows or late cancellations
  - **AI Phone Assistant:** see `dish-ai-phone-assistant.md`
- **Bundle:** DISH Premium (website + Professional Reservation + Professional Order): EUR 79/month (36 months), EUR 89 (12 months), EUR 94 (3 months), setup fee EUR 279.

## Sales notes (project-authored, not from DISH)

- **Pain signals in reviews:**
  - "Called several times, nobody answered" / "impossible to get through by phone"
  - "Can't book online" / "had to call to reserve"
  - "Our reservation was lost" / "they double-booked our table"
  - "Always full on weekends, no way to reserve in advance"
- **Pain signals from the owner side (no-shows):** reviews mentioning empty reserved tables while walk-ins were turned away.
- **Good fit:** Sit-down restaurants that take bookings, especially busy ones.
- **Weak fit:** Walk-in-only places (snack bars, takeaway counters).
- **Possible objection:** "Booking platforms charge me per guest." → Fixed monthly fee, 0% commission per reservation.
